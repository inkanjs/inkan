inkan takes files,
serves your frontend
and packs it.

  • t.binary(): a file as the body itself
  • t.stream(): uploads larger than memory
  • a body limit per route
  • serveStatic: your build, SPA and all
  • compress(): brotli or gzip, streams too
  • ctx.ip, and rate limits that use it
  • @inkanjs 0.2.0: next, vite, query, uws
